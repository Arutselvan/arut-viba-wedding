# Arut & Viba Wedding Website — Design Brainstorm

## Design Philosophy: "Ink & Marigold"

**Design Movement:** Romantic South Asian Editorial — a fusion of Indian wedding opulence with modern editorial minimalism.

**Core Principles:**
1. The calligraphic "AV" logo is the soul — every element echoes its flowing, ink-stroke aesthetic
2. Warmth over coldness: saffron, ivory, and deep charcoal replace sterile blues
3. Storytelling through scroll — the page unfolds like a love letter, not a brochure
4. Asymmetric tension: text and images never sit in predictable grids; they breathe and lean

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Deep ivory / warm parchment (#FAF6F0) — like aged love letters
- Primary accent: Marigold saffron (#E8A020) — the color of Haldi and celebration
- Deep accent: Burnt sienna / terracotta (#C4622D) — warmth and depth
- Text: Rich charcoal (#1C1410) — ink on parchment
- Secondary text: Warm taupe (#7A6552)
- Highlight: Champagne gold (#D4A853) — for borders and ornamental details

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-bleed hero with overlay text, not a centered card
- Horizontal scroll timeline for the love story
- Asymmetric two-column sections alternating image/text sides
- Square photo grid for the couple's memories
- Sticky navigation that transforms on scroll

**Signature Elements:**
1. SVG floral dividers between sections (marigold/jasmine motifs)
2. Animated countdown timer with elegant typography
3. Scroll-triggered fade-in and slide animations via Framer Motion

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Scroll-driven narrative: each section reveals itself as you journey down
- Hover states on photos create a warm golden overlay with captions
- The timeline is horizontally scrollable on desktop, vertical on mobile

**Animation:**
- Entry animations: fade-up with 0.6s ease-out, staggered for lists
- Hero text: letter-by-letter or word-by-word reveal
- Section transitions: gentle parallax on background images
- Countdown: smooth number flip animation

**Typography System:**
- Display/Script: "Cormorant Garamond" (elegant, editorial serif) for names and headings
- Body: "Lato" (clean, readable) for paragraphs
- Accent: The AV logo SVG as the primary brand mark
- Hierarchy: 72px display → 48px h1 → 32px h2 → 18px body

---

**Selected Approach: Ink & Marigold**
This design honors the calligraphic logo's soul while creating a warm, immersive experience that tells Arut & Viba's cross-country love story through elegant scroll-driven storytelling.
